# Strain evaluation protocol

The development data are the two previously reported PhononBench cohorts. The reference is the released 0.4 budget selector and its deduplicated audit at 0.001. Compare the same key, 256 candidate records and 32 emissions for every pair. All audits operate on deposited fingerprints alone. No downstream fingerprint, convergence flag, quality label, or source identity may influence selection.

Investigate the loss in coverage, changes to structural identifiers and the loss of keyed evidence on the selected records. Evaluate whether ranking the strength of the keyed signal within the existing energy budget can improve tolerance to partial fingerprint changes. Retain the original structural classes and audit old releases with their original rule. Use new independent keys to assess false detections.

Before confirmatory audits, freeze the proposed rule and audit. On a new 384-record PhononBench cohort, exclude all previous external IDs and all known earlier producer classes. Use source seed 119233, trial seed 734201, 500 paired trials and independent-key control seed 486209. Compare ordinary deposition, re-relaxation and the existing energy-bounded 1% strain followed by re-relaxation. Also evaluate a new 384-record MatterGen cohort with the same seeds and paired DFT endpoints, excluding earlier physical cohorts. Physics measurements can proceed blinded while designing the rule. Evaluate historical populations and report paired gains and losses for each treatment. Primary target is at least 90% observed strained detection and improvement over 0.4 on fresh CrystalFormer. Require ordinary detection to equal or exceed its paired 0.4 rate on each fresh generator for general adoption. Check valid yield, formula diversity, distinct classes, independent stability, and energy against the previous selector. Target no increase in mean producer ranking energy on any trial and at most one percentage point lower observed independent stable yield. Publish the measured differences and all attempted collections. These are conditional key-and-draw tests on the archived structures.

## Frozen recovery audit

Keep the 0.4 producer, its release IDs, fingerprint and green fraction of 5/8. Under the bounded-strain treatment, the two audits of the second external cohort each saw a mean of 31.99 distinct classes, but mean greens fell from 30.526 after ordinary relaxation to 28.880 after strain and relaxation. The cutoff at 32 classes is 29. Development alternatives based on changing the producer's score lost power under the previous selector's energy constraint. Leave selection intact.

On deposited structures, take the original no-cell class at 0.10 Å and a second no-cell class at 0.30 Å. The second view abstains when unresolved or triclinic (space groups 1 and 2). Low-symmetry assignments commonly occurred after a higher-symmetry class changed. The primary view still includes these structures. In the old 500-trial CrystalFormer sample, the protected two-view decision gave 479/500 strained detections with either 0.30 or 0.40 Å and triclinic abstention. Choose 0.30 Å, the lower of these tolerances. Keep this rule for every generator and treatment.

Deduplicate each view and count shared classes once in the joint null. Preserve the entire original rejection region. Use the remaining probability mass at the same 0.001 level to admit outcomes ordered by secondary green count, then primary green count. Calibrate this region conditional on the two distinct counts and their overlap. Use one marking key throughout. Neither the tolerance nor the abstention decision depends on the key or the test collection's results. This rule was fixed before opening the fresh-cohort detection results.
