# Strain evaluation protocol

The development data are the two previously reported PhononBench cohorts. The reference is the released 0.4 budget selector and its deduplicated audit at 0.001. Compare the same key, 256 candidate records and 32 emissions for every pair. All audits operate on deposited fingerprints alone. No downstream fingerprint, convergence flag, quality label, or source identity may influence selection.

Investigate the loss in coverage, changes to structural identifiers and the loss of keyed evidence on the selected records. Evaluate whether ranking the strength of the keyed signal within the existing energy budget can improve tolerance to partial fingerprint changes. Retain the original structural classes and audit old releases with their original rule. Use new independent keys to assess false detections.

Before confirmatory audits, freeze the proposed rule and audit. On a new 384-record PhononBench cohort, exclude all previous external IDs and all known earlier producer classes. Use source seed 119233, trial seed 734201, 500 paired trials and independent-key control seed 486209. Compare ordinary deposition, re-relaxation and the existing energy-bounded 1% strain followed by re-relaxation. Also evaluate a new 384-record MatterGen cohort with the same seeds and paired DFT endpoints, excluding earlier physical cohorts. Physics measurements can proceed blinded while designing the rule. Evaluate historical populations and report paired gains and losses for each treatment. Primary target is at least 90% observed strained detection and improvement over 0.4 on fresh CrystalFormer. Require ordinary detection to equal or exceed its paired 0.4 rate on each fresh generator for general adoption. Check valid yield, formula diversity, distinct classes, independent stability, and energy against the previous selector. Target no increase in mean producer ranking energy on any trial and at most one percentage point lower observed independent stable yield. Publish the measured differences and all attempted collections. These are conditional key-and-draw tests on the archived structures.

## Frozen recovery audit

Keep the 0.4 producer, its release IDs, fingerprint and green fraction of 5/8. Under the bounded-strain treatment, the two audits of the second external cohort each saw a mean of 31.99 distinct classes, but mean greens fell from 30.526 after ordinary relaxation to 28.880 after strain and relaxation. The cutoff at 32 classes is 29. Development alternatives based on changing the producer's score lost power under the previous selector's energy constraint. Leave selection intact.

On deposited structures, take the original no-cell class at 0.10 Å and a second no-cell class at 0.30 Å. The second view abstains when unresolved or triclinic (space groups 1 and 2). Low-symmetry assignments commonly occurred after a higher-symmetry class changed. The primary view still includes these structures. In the old 500-trial CrystalFormer sample, the protected two-view decision gave 479/500 strained detections with either 0.30 or 0.40 Å and triclinic abstention. Choose 0.30 Å, the lower of these tolerances. Keep this rule for every generator and treatment.

Deduplicate each view and count shared classes once in the joint null. Preserve the entire original rejection region. Use the remaining probability mass at the same 0.001 level to admit outcomes ordered by secondary green count, then primary green count. Calibrate this region conditional on the two distinct counts and their overlap. Use one marking key throughout. Neither the tolerance nor the abstention decision depends on the key or the test collection's results. This rule was fixed before opening the fresh-cohort detection results.

## Additional physical challenge

Apply a previously unevaluated simple shear to the same fresh PhononBench structures using the identity matrix with entry (1, 2) set to 0.015 (one-based indices), left-multiplied into the source lattice at fixed fractional coordinates. Accept only valid proposals with absolute change in MACE-OMAT energy at most 10 meV/atom. Relax from the accepted geometry, otherwise from the original, and deposit using the existing procedure. Run the same frozen decisions, keys and energy-matched selections. This is an additional channel; keep the axial strain test as the primary endpoint.

## Follow-up on energy rank

On the first fresh cohort the frozen recovery audit improved strained detection from 299/500 to 414/500, below the target. Even before treatment the original decision fired on 453/500 releases. The selector includes globally cheap fallback candidates when constrained by its mean energy budget. Check whether rank computed independently from deposited coordinates can identify structures carrying more signal. Development may now include the first confirmation cohort. Never substitute the unreleased producer energies for deposited scores in a reported deployment test. Retain all preceding results.

Reserve two further PhononBench groups in the next draw (source seed 201773), excluding every older external ID and producer class including the first confirmation cohort. Take 768 source structures, allocate the first and last 384 to separate evaluation cohorts, and check cross-cohort overlap. Use 500 independent trials per cohort with seeds 931153 and 957203, paired across policies. Keep the original 32/256 design and all previous energy and audit settings. Finalize the new recovery decision before inspecting any detection result for either group. Report every cohort separately.

**Locked rank extension.** Keep every rejection of the frozen primary and two-view tests. For at most 32 distinct classes in each view, use an additional subset of the recovery classes. Sort distinct primary classes by a MACE-OMAT single-point energy at the deposited coordinates minus the pinned LeMat composition hull. Keep the upper three quarters, dropping floor(K/4) at the low end. For repeated primary classes use the lowest energy then the lexicographically first recovery class as the representative. Count each retained recovery class once. Add previously unrejected outcomes in decreasing order of subset green count, secondary green count and primary green count until the next inclusion would exceed the nominal level. The joint null shares one Bernoulli label per distinct class across all three sets. Use the earlier two-view decision for larger collections. Geometry-only audits remain available without model or reference files. Retain both the geometry-only and ranked results. This design was fixed before either reserved group's audit. If a producer class crosses the reserved split, retain all its members in the first group and swap an equal count of last-in-order first-half singleton classes to preserve two disjoint groups of 384.

## Secondary release-size comparison

After the first 32-output reserved result, evaluate a separately chosen release size of 40 on the two reserved groups and the first confirmation cohort. Compare six candidates per output (240 total) and eight per output (320 total). Use the existing budget selector with green probability 5/8, the geometry-only recovery audit, the same paired 500 seeds per cohort and the 0.001 level. The primary 32/256 results stay in the main comparison. Treat the size analysis as secondary and report both budgets, physical yield and absolute energy. These compare separate prespecified releases; testing several sizes on the same disputed collection would require its own joint calibration.
