# Fixed-budget confirmation protocol

Use the previously published A and B cohorts and earlier observations for development. Reserve two further groups of 384 CrystalFormer PhononBench structures using source seed 826391 and the published class-disjoint splitter. Exclude the historical Figshare classes, both 0.4 external cohorts, the first 0.5 confirmation cohort and both halves of its reserved draw. Test 500 paired keys and candidate draws per new cohort using seeds 271573 and 862317, with 20 independent control keys per collection from seeds 413833 and 739021. Archive all trials. No detection results on either new group may inform the method choice.

The primary endpoint is structure-only detection after the existing bounded 1% axial strain, independent relaxation, standardization and five-decimal CIF deposition. Each release uses exactly 32 outputs from 256 candidates, the 0.4 energy budget and a collection false-positive level of 0.001 under the key-independent null. Success requires strictly more than 450 detections out of 500 on each fresh cohort. Report each cohort separately, including paired gains, losses, uncertainty and independent controls. Check ordinary processing, the previous shear treatment, archived MatterGen outcomes, the earlier baseline positives, and changes to material quality and costs. Keep development observations separate from confirmation. Freeze the implementation before opening either fresh detection result.
