# Fixed-budget collection audit

A survival-ranked audit reads the deposited symmetry views and deposited energies and retains earlier detections on the same collection. With 32 emissions and 256 candidates, it detected 385/500 and 490/500 strained CrystalFormer releases in two prospective groups. On the same ordinary re-relaxations it detected 487/500 and 495/500. The two fresh groups met the 90% target on ordinary processing. Only the second met it after strain. [Frozen design](protocol.md)

A separate removal experiment used 500 releases on the earlier first-confirmation cohort. Ordinary relaxation gave 489 detections. A binary-feedback attacker could try up to eight prespecified collections of the same crystals, using a bounded shear. With the validity and paired energy checks, 474 remained detectable. Restricting edits to crystals passing the paired harmonic stability screen at both geometries left 487 detectable. The CHGNet-r2SCAN screen used finite displacements and a 4 by 4 by 4 frequency mesh on all 85 distinct eligible emitted crystals. Both endpoints passed on 11 pairs, one lost a pass, three gained a pass and 70 failed both.

From the repository root, run `python scripts/benchmark_reliability.py --case C` and repeat for D to reproduce the prospective audit. Use `python scripts/benchmark_bounded_removal.py --verify results/v0.6/bounded-removal.json.gz` for the energy-bounded removal arm. Add `--phonons results/v0.6/emitted-removal-phonons.json.gz --rule both-pass` and verify against `results/v0.6/removal-both-pass.json.gz` for the stricter outcome.
